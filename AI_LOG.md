## Human/student evidence vs. test evidence

- The original sensor dataset is the **instructor-provided Module 6 sample CSV**, not a new physical sensor reading.
- The original Module 6 SQLite history is preserved and identified by source and timestamp fields.
- Elevated temperatures and missing readings in demonstration mode are **software-injected test stimuli**, explicitly tagged `TEST_INJECTED`.
- Real workflow verification is deterministic Python/SQLite/HTTP assertion-based. Dashboard screenshots, when generated, come from a **real browser connected to the running Flask server**, not from AI-generated images.

AI did not measure physical temperatures, prove instrument calibration, or determine what happened during missing Module 6 time gaps. All numerical rules are documented classroom thresholds and are tested in code.
